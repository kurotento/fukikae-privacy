Fukikae Uploader

Fukikae Uploader is a private command-line tool used by one person: the owner of a YouTube channel that publishes Japanese-dubbed versions of English videos released under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license.

Uploads to the owner's YouTube channel: UCtdBFSRe-E0ZuW3NM5n6CMg

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the owner's channel and schedules its publish time (YouTube Data API: videos.insert)
  • Sets the video thumbnail (thumbnails.set)
  • Posts a comment on the owner's own video (commentThreads.insert)

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to other users and has no sign-up.
